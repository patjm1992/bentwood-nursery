Shagbark Hickory
(Carya ovata)
From productive and cold hardy trees in upstate NY. Zones 4-8.

Beach Plum
(Prunus maritima)
Seedlings of the most productive shrub in my neighborhood. Great for fresh eating, but most prized in jams and pies. Native to Maine, but supposedly endangered. Zone 3-8.
$10   bare root
Siberian C Peach
(Prunus persica)
Seedlings grown from seed sourced from Vermont (Zone 4!). A hardy peach that is supposedly brown rot resistant.
$10   bare root
American Elderberry
(Sambucus canadensis)
Rooted cuttings of a vigorous and productive shrub in my landscape, Native. Limited amount of ‘Bob Gordon’ cultivar elders. Also some untagged cultivars in the mix that originally came from Edible Acres nursery. Zone 4.
$12   bare root
American (?) Chestnut
(Castanea dentata)
I can’t prove they’re all-American without a genetics test, but they have all the American characteristics. Collected from populations in Hancock county and midcoast. Zones 4-8.
$15   bare root
Hybrid Chestnut
(Castanea spp.)
From seed source, Twisted Tree Farm: “One of the best strains of chestnut anywhere in the world. Seed for these trees are collected from select trees in a local established chestnut orchard in upstate NY. The trees are blight resistant and produce large beautiful chestnuts. They are open pollinated and seedlings may contain genes from American, Chinese, European, and Japanese chestnuts. Some of the mother trees include such cultivars as 'Willamette', 'Armstrong', 'Jersey Gem', 'Eaton', 'Mossbarger', as well as many other outstanding hybrids. This is our most proven strain of chestnut and the best for northern climates. Hardy to zone 4.”
$15   bare root
Black Walnut
(Juglans nigra)
Rarely found in downeast Maine, but seed collected from MDI. One of the most Native to the eastern US. Zone 4.
$10   bare root
American Persimmon
(Diospyros virginiana)
Seed from Twisted Tree Farm, proven cold hardy genetics. Native to the eastern US. Zone 5, possibly 4.
$12   bare root
Pawpaw
(Asimina triloba)
Seed from Twisted Tree Farm in NY and some from Maine (I have noted which are which, just ask if you have a preference). North America’s largest native fruit! So tropical and most are hooked from the first bite, myself included. I am planting as many of these in downeast Maine as I can manage. Zone 4/5.
$15   bare root sold out!
Nanking Cherry
(Prunus tomentosa)
Shrub cherry native to northern and western China. Zone 2-7.
$12   bare root
Hybrid Hazelnut
(Corylus spp.)
From seed sourced, Twisted Tree Farm: “Seed gathered from extremely productive hybrid hazel bushes here at Twisted Tree Farm. The mother plants originate largely from several breeding programs in the midwest and northeast. We have been planting hazelnuts from as many different genetic sources as possible since 2009. These are very tough plants that produce huge crops at a young age. Hardy to zone 3 and resistant to eastern filbert blight.”
$12   bare root
Antonovka Apple / Антоновка обыкновенная
(Malus pumila)
Mega cold hardy seedling apple tree legendary in Russia. It has a reputation for being true to seed, which is unusual for apples, and is sometimes used to produce vigorous, standard sized rootstocks from seed, but I’ll be growing them for their beautiful yellow apples that store well. Zone 3. More info at this Wikipedia article.
