Why Bentwood?

Bentwood is a small, hands-on nursery built around a simple idea: grow useful plants.

I'm interested in trees and perennial plants that belong in real gardens, food forests, hedgerows and little backyard experiments — especially plants that are tough enough for Maine and interesting enough to make you want to plant one.

No giant catalog. No fancy garden-center markup. Just plants being propagated and grown on a small scale.
